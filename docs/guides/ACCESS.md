# Limiting people to some workspaces

By default everyone in an organization sees all of its workspaces. For an agency, a workspace is usually a client, and a client (or a contractor) should see their own and nothing else.

## How to use it

**Team page → a member's menu → Workspace access…** Switch on "Limit to some workspaces" and tick the ones they may see. Save. They see only those workspaces in the switcher, the overview, every list and every link; the others don't exist as far as they can tell.

**When inviting:** choose a viewer or member, switch on the limit and tick the workspaces before sending. The limit is recorded first and applied the moment the invitation is accepted, so the person never has a moment of full access.

Switch the limit off to give someone every workspace again.

## Rules

- Only **viewers and members** can be limited. Admins and owners always see everything, because they manage the organization. A limited person can't be made an admin or owner until the limit is lifted.
- A limited person with no workspace ticked sees nothing, and is told to ask an admin.
- Only admins can change any of this. A limited person can't lift their own limit.
- It is available on every plan.

## What a limited person can still see

- The **Team** page lists the organization's members (names, emails and roles), because that comes from the sign-in system rather than from a workspace. If that matters for a client, invite them as a member of a separate organization instead.
- **Plan and usage** figures count the whole organization (a limit on the plan is a limit on the organization, whoever creates the monitor).

## What it does not do

- Public status pages are public by design and don't depend on who is signed in.
- Removing someone from the organization keeps their recorded limit, so inviting them again later does not widen what they can see. An admin can change it from the Team page.
